About Iqranix

Your Trusted Islamic Companion

Welcome to Iqranix, a modern Islamic companion created to help Muslims strengthen their relationship with Allah ﷻ through authentic knowledge, daily worship, and meaningful remembrance.

Inspired by the very first revelation of the Holy Qur'an, Iqranix combines beautiful design with trusted Islamic resources to create a simple, elegant, and spiritually enriching experience.

Whether you are reading the Qur'an, listening to recitation, learning authentic duas, checking prayer times, or remembering Allah through daily dhikr, Iqranix is designed to accompany you throughout your journey of faith.

Our Mission

Bringing Muslims Closer to Allah

Our mission is to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible, beautiful, and beneficial for every Muslim around the world.

Every feature in Iqranix is carefully designed to encourage worship, learning, remembrance of Allah ﷻ, and a deeper understanding of Islam through modern technology.

Our Vision

A Trusted Digital Islamic Companion

We envision Iqranix becoming one of the world's most trusted Islamic applications by combining authenticity, simplicity, elegant design, and meaningful technology.

Our goal is to support Muslims of every age and background in their daily worship and lifelong journey of seeking knowledge.
