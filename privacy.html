Welcome to Iqranix.

At Iqranix, we believe that privacy is more than a legal responsibility—it is an Amanah (trust). Islam teaches us to uphold honesty, integrity, fairness, and respect for the rights of others. These principles guide the way we build and maintain our application.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage information while you use Iqranix.

"Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due."

— Surah An-Nisa (4:58)
1. Information We Collect

Privacy by Design

Iqranix is designed with the principle of Privacy by Design. We collect only the information necessary to provide the services you choose to use and to improve your experience within the application.

Depending on how you use Iqranix, we may collect:

  • Device language and region.
  • Prayer time calculation preferences.
  • Application settings and personalization preferences.
  • Bookmarks and saved content stored on your device.
  • Anonymous diagnostic information used to improve performance and reliability.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

2. Location Services

Used Only When You Choose

Some features require access to your device's location in order to function correctly.

These features include:

  • Prayer Times
  • Qiblah Direction
  • Nearby Mosques

Your location is used only to provide these features and is not intended to monitor, record, or track your movements.

You may disable location access at any time through your device settings. Doing so may limit the functionality of certain features.

3. Qur'an & Islamic Content

Authentic Knowledge

Iqranix provides access to the Holy Qur'an, audio recitations, translations, tafsir, duas, and other Islamic educational resources.

We strive to use trusted and authentic sources wherever possible. However, users are encouraged to seek guidance from qualified Islamic scholars when making personal religious decisions.

Our objective is to make authentic Islamic knowledge more accessible while maintaining respect for the diversity of recognised scholarly opinions.

4. How We Use Information

Using Information Responsibly

Any information collected through Iqranix is used solely to provide, maintain, and improve the services offered within the application.

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Islamic features that you choose to use.
  • Remember your application preferences and settings.
  • Improve performance, reliability, and stability.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical issues.
  • Protect the security and integrity of Iqranix.

We do not use your information for purposes inconsistent with this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage

Where Your Information Is Stored

Much of your information, including bookmarks, preferences, and application settings, is stored locally on your device.

Some features may securely communicate with trusted online services to retrieve Qur'an content, prayer times, translations, recitations, and other Islamic resources.

We aim to minimise the amount of information stored or transmitted whenever possible.

6. Data Sharing

We Respect Your Privacy

Iqranix does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be shared when necessary to:

  • Operate features you choose to use.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Protect the safety, rights, and security of users and the application.

Where trusted third-party services are used, they are expected to process information only for the purpose of providing their services.

7. Security

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to safeguard information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

While we work continuously to maintain a secure environment, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We continually review and improve our security practices as Iqranix evolves.

8. Children's Privacy

Protecting Young Users

Iqranix is designed to benefit Muslims of all ages, including children and families.

We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of internet-enabled applications and guide them in using digital services responsibly.

9. Your Rights

Your Choices Matter

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

  • Access information relating to you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information where applicable.
  • Request deletion of information where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw permissions such as location access through your device settings at any time.
10. Updates to This Policy

Keeping You Informed

As Iqranix continues to grow, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect new features, improvements, operational changes, or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available within the application. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your privacy while using Iqranix, please contact us.
